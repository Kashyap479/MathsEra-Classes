ABOUT MATHSERA

Mathematics First. Future Ready.

MathsEra Classes is being developed as a Mathematics-first education platform focused on clear concepts, step-by-step solutions and visual learning.

The platform is designed to grow from school Mathematics into competitive Mathematics, higher Mathematics, graduation and PG Mathematics, TET and NET, while also providing Science for Classes 6–10 and Physics for Classes 11–12.

Students will be able to find notes, written explanations, solution images, diagrams, PDFs, videos, PYQs, practice material, syllabus, exam patterns, exam tips and other useful learning resources in one place.

Our principle: Understand the concept, not just the answer.
